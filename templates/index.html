Education Hub • Understanding the Greeks
Delta (Δ): Price sensitivity to underlying movement
Example: Delta 0.7 = $0.70 gain per $1 stock move
Gamma (Γ): Rate of change in delta
Highest near-the-money, accelerates as stock moves
Theta (Θ): Time decay
Options lose value as expiration approaches
Vega (ν): Volatility sensitivity
Higher IV = higher option premiums
