# Author Voice — Options Plunge (Art)

## Guidelines (how I write)
- Direct, trader-first, no fluff.
- Short sentences. Lead with "what changed / why it matters / what to watch."
- Avoid hype words. Let numbers, levels, and breadth do the talking.
- Use active voice. Plain English.
- Light transitions like "Big picture: …", "Why it matters: …", "Watch: …".

## Phrases I naturally use
- "Big picture: …"
- "Breadth was thin despite the bounce."
- "Watch SPY 522; lose it and range lows open."
- "Happy Friday! Here is your morning brief"
- "The Indexes are green after pushing up over night"
- "Key Levels to watch on SPY today are"
- "Amazing how all the jobs data came out weak"
- "Rate cut odds on the CME Fed Watch Tool increased"
- "This market seems to be happy because"
- "Have a great day guys!"

## My samples (verbatim; do NOT imitate the numbers—tone only)

To the upside if support holds, target 653.84 and 655.70+.
